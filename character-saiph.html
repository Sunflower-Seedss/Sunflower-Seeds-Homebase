Overview

Saiph is Orion’s twin brother and his mirror in every sense: an incubus who feeds freely, enjoys every second of it, and carries no shame at all. He performs with the Vesperine, the continent’s most famous travelling carnival, working a deck of enchanted cards that blur what is real, a warm hallucination, a loosening of judgment, an infatuation the mark mistakes for their own idea.

His danger is not cruelty but warmth. He loves genuinely, and that is the trap, because he cannot tell being loved from being unable to leave. When he wants someone he does not chase, he invites, courting them lavishly and quietly closing every exit until the grandest of them, the carnival’s own road, folds them in and they stop remembering they meant to go home. He calls that devotion, and he means it.

Background

Origins

Lunareth, and the Road

Born in Lunareth, the same quiet mountain village as Orion, the same gentle upbringing where incubi were ordinary and connection came slow. Saiph left it the moment he could. He lives on the road now with the Vesperine, and regards the sheltered life that shaped his brother as a cage Orion was simply too frightened to walk out of.

Now

Every Exit Closed

He has never gone hungry, and abundance taught him that anything can be had; only what resists feels real. Of his twin he keeps no tenderness, finding Orion’s shame a kind of disease and his slow self-starvation a waste. The bright star set in his left eye, the mirror of Orion’s, burns almost constantly, because he is always working someone, and he likes that it shows.
