DreamJourney Community Event

Summer '26

A limited-time seasonal event built around a summer tag, an exclusive bot card skin, and collectible badges.

01 How It Works

A broad “summer vacation” theme, open to beach trips, road trips, staycations, seasonal slice-of-life, and anything else creators dream up.

  • The Tag. A time-gated Summer ’26 tag joins the library for the season, selectable from roughly 21 June to 22 September. Outside that window it stays visible on existing bots but can’t be added to new ones.
  • Custom Card Skin. Any bot carrying the tag gets the Summer ’26 treatment, a warm tint, lemon pill tags and a themed hover glow, replacing the default card style for the event window.
  • Profile Toggle. A toggle on profile pages lets people hide or show event-skinned cards in their grid. Opt-out, off by default, for anyone who’d rather keep their curated look.
  • Badges. Two ways to earn a keepsake, one for creators and one for players, displayed in the profile badge collection.

03 The Badges

Earned during the event window and kept on your profile afterward.

Creator

Creator Badge

Awarded for publishing a bot with the Summer ’26 tag during the event. One badge per creator, however many bots you make.

Player

Participant Badge

Awarded for sending 50 messages across event-tagged bots, so players have a reason to dive in too.
