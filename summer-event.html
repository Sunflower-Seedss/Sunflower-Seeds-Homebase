DreamJourney Community Event

Summer Heat

A 30-day summer celebration. Tag a bot, earn exclusive badges and credits, and keep them on your profile for good. Runs 22 June to 22 July.

01 What You Can Earn

Join in during the 30 days to collect exclusive badges that stay on your profile for good, plus a credit bonus for creators. The more you take part, the more you keep.

For Creators

Sunshine

Publish a safe-for-work bot with the Summer Heat tag to earn it. One badge per creator, however many bots you make.

For Readers

Vacationer

Spend time with Summer Heat bots to earn it, so anyone enjoying the event can collect a keepsake of their own.

+500 credits A bonus for every creator’s first bot published with the Summer Heat tag, on top of the badge and the card skin.

02 How It Works

A broad “summer vacation” theme, open to beach trips, road trips, staycations, seasonal slice-of-life, and anything else you dream up.

  • 30 days only. The Summer Heat tag runs from Monday 22 June to 22 July. When it closes, the tag can no longer be added to new bots and the special card skin switches off, but every badge you earned stays on your profile for good. Jump in while it lasts.
  • Tag your bot. Add the Summer Heat tag to any safe-for-work bot to enter it into the event. SFW bots only.
  • A seasonal card skin. Every tagged bot wears the exclusive Summer Heat card treatment for the length of the event, a warm tint, lemon pill tags and a themed glow.
