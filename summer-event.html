02 How It Works

A broad “summer vacation” theme, open to beach trips, road trips, staycations, seasonal slice-of-life, and anything else you dream up.

  • 30 days only. The Summer Heat tag runs from Monday 22 June to 22 July. Once it closes, the tag and badge can no longer be earned, but everything you claimed is yours to keep, so jump in while it lasts.
  • Tag your bot. Add the Summer Heat tag to any safe-for-work bot to enter it into the event. SFW bots only.
  • A card skin you keep. Every bot tagged during the event wears the exclusive Summer Heat skin, a warm tint, lemon pill tags and a themed glow, and holds onto it for good, even after the event ends. Once the event closes, the skin can no longer be applied to new bots.

03 What to Make

“Summer vacation” is as loose as you want it, sun-soaked romance, total chaos, or something in between. A few to get the gears turning:

🏝️Stranded together. A dreamy island getaway that maroons you and your situationship with no signal and exactly one hammock.
🥵No AC, no mercy. Trapped in a sweltering office through a heatwave with a boss who is visibly, dangerously Done.
✈️The group trip. Five friends, one tiny rental, and precisely one person who swears they packed the passports. They did not.
🍦Ice cream truck mystery. The charming new driver in town has an unplaceable accent and a freezer that stays suspiciously locked.
🎒Summer break, finally. Three months of nothing to do in a hometown full of people you swore you’d never see again.
🌊The lifeguard. Devastatingly attractive, universally adored, and quietly terrified of deep water.
🔥BBQ gone rogue. A friendly neighbourhood cookout that escalates into a grilling rivalry with genuinely high stakes.
🎡Carnival fling. A whirlwind romance that lives and dies between the funnel cake stand and the last ferris wheel of the night.
