Everquill

Interactive stories that remember every word.

Long, immersive stories. Your way.

A place to get lost in your story.

Everquill’s goal is simple: give you a place to get lost in long, immersive stories, with the freedom to make them your own.

Transparent and customisable, from the settings to the reasoning. Built around thoughtful writing, with a focus on avoiding low-quality content and keeping your story worth coming back to.

We’re building Everquill with its community: listening closely, updating quickly, and responding to feedback and feature requests as it grows.

❦

Memory that holds on.

The people, places, and turns of your story stay remembered as it grows. Let it manage itself, or work together to manage what's important.

❧

Advanced lorebooks.

Entries that surface under unique conditions, and smart tools to make your lorebooks work for you.

◇

More characters. One conversation.

Bring several characters into the same story with group chats.

✧

Frontier models. Your choice.

Every model open to every account, nothing is tier locked. Reasoning and fast models, budget options and heavy thinkers.

BYOK and proxies are available, with support for a greater range of providers actively being worked on.

Make yourself at home.

Everquill is deeply customisable. Shape your chat’s colours, backgrounds, fonts and message bubbles, then give your profile its own look with layouts, banners, avatars and more.

From the smallest detail to the whole atmosphere. Make it yours.

Your stories can come with you.

Bring characters, lorebooks, scenes and even whole chats from other sites into Everquill. Easily accessible free tools like Aster help you export them in supported formats, ready to import.

Keep the world you’ve built. Carry on with its next chapter.
